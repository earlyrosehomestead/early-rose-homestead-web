Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How should I store my microgreens?

Keep them refrigerated and as dry as possible in their original food-safe container. Avoid leaving the container open or allowing excess moisture to collect inside.

Should I wash microgreens before eating them?

Yes. Wash the amount you plan to use under clean, cool running water and gently dry before serving.

How long will they keep?

Freshness varies by variety and storage conditions. Use the Best By information on your package as your guide and discard greens that show signs of spoilage.

Where are Early Rose microgreens grown?

Our microgreens are grown by Early Rose Homestead in Michigan with an emphasis on freshness, careful handling, and small-batch growing.

Do you offer subscriptions?

Subscription options may be available. Contact us and we can discuss current varieties, frequency, and fulfillment options.

Do you deliver or ship?

Fulfillment options depend on your location and current availability. Contact us with your location and what you would like to order, and we will confirm the options available for that order.

Can I request a specific variety?

Absolutely. Tell us what you are looking for. If it is not currently ready, we can let you know whether it is part of an upcoming grow.

Why Early Rose?

Early Rose began with a simple idea: grow nutritious food, know where it comes from, and build something meaningful from the ground up.

What started as a desire to grow more of our own food has grown into a small homestead business centered on fresh, thoughtfully grown microgreens and the belief that the simple things are often worth doing exceptionally well.
